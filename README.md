# Employee_Attrition_Analysis

Employee Attrition Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive employee attrition analytics dashboard developed in Power BI to examine workforce turnover across organizational, demographic, and behavioral dimensions.
The dashboard is designed to support exploratory analysis and decision-making, allowing users to quickly identify patterns, concentration areas, and contributing factors related to attrition.

Project Focus

Quantifying overall attrition through key workforce metrics

Identifying roles, departments, and job levels with higher attrition exposure

Examining how overtime, business travel, and compensation patterns relate to employee exits

Enabling targeted analysis through dynamic filtering of employee segments

Key Analytics Covered

Attrition Rate and Workforce KPIs: headcount, average age, tenure, and monthly income

Department- and Role-level Attrition: comparison across R&D, Sales, HR, and job roles

Job Level and Education Field Analysis: identifying attrition concentration points

Behavioral Indicators: overtime participation and travel frequency vs attrition

Compensation Signals: monthly income and percent salary hike distribution for attrited vs retained employees

Age-based Trend: attrition variation across age groups

Interactivity

Filters for department, gender, job role, education field, and age range

All metrics and visuals update dynamically based on selected segments

Enables focused, scenario-based attrition analysis without rebuilding reports

Tools

Power BI Desktop

DAX-based measures

HR analytics dataset

Why This Project Matters

This dashboard emphasizes analytical clarity and decision relevance over static reporting.
It demonstrates the ability to translate HR data into structured, actionable insights, reflecting how attrition analysis is applied in real organizational settings.
